# 05 — Marketing Plan

Two funnels, two audiences, one brand. Student marketing is Bangla, Facebook/YouTube/WhatsApp-led. Client marketing is English, LinkedIn/Upwork/Clutch/email-led. Never mix them in one post.

---

## 1. Student funnel (Academy)

```
Reels/Shorts + YouTube + FB group answers  ->  Free orientation (live monthly) or recorded
        ->  WhatsApp list  ->  Selection test  ->  Enrol (gateway)  ->  Batch  ->  Results page
                                                         ^                               |
                                                         +---- success stories, cohort numbers
```

### Content pillars (student side), with a target ratio
| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Teach** (short, one problem, one fix) | 40% | "QBO-তে Undeposited Funds কেন বাড়ে", "Xero bank rule ৩ মিনিটে", "VAT return-এ এই ভুলটা সবাই করে" |

| **Behind the work** | 15% | A cleanup day on a real (anonymised) file, Noman reviewing a student's reconciliation, a UK VAT quarter close |

Repurpose rule: one long video a week becomes 3 Shorts, 2 Reels, 1 LinkedIn post, 1 FB text post, 1 WhatsApp tip. One recording session, seven pieces.

### Free orientation (the conversion event)
- Live, monthly, 60–75 min, Bangla, Zoom. Cap at 150. Recorded version on the site for the rest of the month.
- Structure: 10 min the honest market (with the research numbers), 25 min a live task in QBO (bank reconciliation that does not balance), 10 min how the course and the agency work, 10 min results so far, 10 min Q&A, then the selection test link and the early-bird deadline.
- Expected conversion (estimate, no BD source): 100 attendees -> 15–25 take the test -> 10–15 pay. So a 30-seat batch needs 200–300 registrations, roughly 2 orientations or 1 orientation plus a strong retargeting week.

### Paid ads (student side)
- Budget per batch: **Tk 40,000–60,000** (research 21 estimates Tk 1.2–6 lakh for 100 students; we want 30).
- Objective: leads to WhatsApp (click-to-WhatsApp) and orientation registrations, not page likes, not "boost post".
- Audiences: Dhaka/Chattogram/Sylhet, 21–32, interests: QuickBooks, Xero, ACCA, CMA, BBA, Bdjobs; lookalike of the WhatsApp list once it reaches 500.
- Creative: the Reality pillar outperforms "learn and earn" with serious students; test both.
- Measure: cost per orientation registration (target under Tk 150), cost per paid student (target under **Tk 2,000**; at Tk 11,000 fee that is under 20% CAC). Kill any ad set above Tk 300 per registration after Tk 5,000 spent.

### Partnerships for students (cheap reach)
- University accounting departments and clubs (NU colleges, private universities): a free 60-min seminar per semester; offer 2 scholarship seats per batch.
- ICMAB/ICAB student groups: Hamidur's CMA and the NSDA L4 open doors.
- LCBS Dhaka already hosted Hamidur: propose a co-branded batch or referral fee.

## 2. Client funnel (Agency)

```
Upwork agency (reviews)  +  Dhaka partner firms (subcontract)  +  Clutch/Google reviews
        ->  LinkedIn + email outreach to ONE niche  ->  2-week trial  ->  seat contract
```

Order of channels (research 19, unchanged): Upwork agency -> subcontract for Dhaka firms -> Clutch -> narrow outbound -> Xero partner programme -> Facebook groups (US/AU bookkeepers) -> QuickBooks directory -> conferences.

### Niche choice (pick one for the first 6 months)
Option A: **UK accounting practices with 5–49 staff on Xero** (7,750 targets; UK-focused BD firms are the most active; Hamidur's syllabus already covers UK VAT/payroll).
Option B: **US e-commerce sellers on QBO with WooCommerce/Shopify** (Ariful's integration edge; Fiverr shows demand with only 2 real integration sellers).
Recommendation: **run A for seats and B for cleanups/integrations**, because they use different channels (LinkedIn/email vs Upwork/Fiverr) and do not compete for the same hour. Do not add a third.

### LinkedIn (client side)
- Company page + three founder profiles with accounting headlines. Ariful's fixes from research 24.
- Founders post 2–3 times a week in English: a cleanup lesson, a UK VAT note, an automation (ERP/integration) story, a cohort result. Comment on 10 UK/US accounting-firm owners' posts a day.
- Outbound: Sales Navigator or Apollo list of the niche; 100 connection requests a week per founder with a one-line note; expect ~3.5 replies per 100 (research 19). One offer only: "a trained Xero bookkeeper, reviewed by a CMA, $X a month, 2-week free trial".
- Email: 300–500 a week to the same list, 4-step sequence, under 80 words each. Expect 3.4% replies.

### Upwork and Fiverr
- Agency account; Noman's prepared profile and gigs go live under it (the build is done; see `fiverr/300_noman-quickbooks`).
- Hamidur's profile joins as a senior (10+ years claimed, certifications shown).
- Fiverr: the four drafted gigs plus one integration gig (WooCommerce/Shopify × QBO) owned by Ariful.
- Goal: 5–10 accounting reviews in 90 days, even on small jobs.

### Proof assets to build in the first 90 days
- 3 case studies (one cleanup, one monthly, one integration), each one page, with before/after numbers.
- 5 reviews on Upwork, 3 on Clutch, 5 on Google.
- A 2-minute English video: who we are, how review works, security.

## 3. Brand-level content that serves both funnels
- **Quarterly results report** (academy numbers + agency growth) as a web page and a PDF. This is the single most differentiated marketing asset in the Bangladesh market.
- **The research itself**: publish a cleaned, public version of `../research/23-whole-market-map.md` as "The State of Accounting Outsourcing from Bangladesh, 2026". Nobody has written it. It positions the brand as the honest expert and earns backlinks and press.

## 5. Things to stop doing
- Boosting posts without a landing page.
- Announcing discounts in inboxes.
- 60-minute YouTube videos.
- Posting Windows tricks and software downloads on the instructor's channel.
- "Learn and earn" copy. We sell work, review and results.
