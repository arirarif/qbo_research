# 03 — Business Model and Phases

Principle (from research file 14, unchanged): **client acquisition first, course second. The course is the hiring and selection funnel, not the product.** The product is (a) accounting work delivered to foreign clients and (b) trained, pre-screened people supplied to employers.

---

## 1. The three engines

```
ENGINE A  Agency (revenue now)         ENGINE B  Academy (funnel + cash)       ENGINE C  Placement (the promise)
Upwork/Fiverr agency + direct clients  Phase 1 Fundamentals course            Own agency seats
Cleanup, monthly books, UK Xero/VAT,   Phase 2 Marketplace Hunting cohort     Partner firms in Dhaka/Sylhet
e-commerce integrations                Phase 3 Placement track                Local SMEs (Tally + QBO)
Owner: Ariful (sales) + Noman/Hamidur  Owner: Hamidur (teach) + Ariful (mkt)  Owner: Ariful (partners) + Noman (QA)
```

Each engine feeds the next: agency work gives students real files and reviews; the best students become agency staff; agency case studies and placed students are the marketing for the next batch. That is the "task renewal" loop from the voice note.

## 3. Phases of the student journey

### Phase 1 — Fundamentals (Hamidur's existing course, upgraded)
| Item | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Who gets in | anyone | **Selection test** (30-min online: debits/credits, a trial balance, an English paragraph). Accept B.Com/BBA/M.Com/CA-CMA students and working juniors. Refuse people who fail the basics, or route them to a cheap recorded "Accounting Basics" pre-course. |
| Length | 20 classes, ~40 h, 10 weeks | **24 classes, ~48 h, 8 weeks** (3 per week) + a graded project |
| Content | software walk-throughs | Keep the QBO + Xero split. Add: **2 messy practice files** (a US e-commerce cleanup, a UK VAT quarter), month-end close checklist, client-communication in English (templates, Loom updates), AI tools for categorisation and review (what the client will expect) |
| Assessment | profile + proposal | **Graded cleanup project reviewed by Noman**, scored 0–100. Score decides Phase 2 entry. |
| Price | Tk 8,000 | **Tk 12,000** regular, Tk 10,000 early-bird, in two instalments. Still under FinTech BPO (25–33k) and Abacus (15k) but no longer priced like a workshop. |
| Batch size | unknown | Target **25–35** paid per batch, 4 batches a year (plan batches to finish Aug–Oct for the local hiring season [18]) |
| Output | certificate | Certificate + score + a portfolio of 2 reviewed project files |

### Phase 2 — Marketplace Hunting cohort (Ariful's phase; the differentiator)
- Entry: top **10–15** from Phase 1 by score, plus an interview. Not sold to everyone. Fee **Tk 15,000** or **free in exchange for a 6-month agency work agreement** (student chooses).
- 6 weeks, hands-on, in small groups:
  - Profiles built with the Noman playbook (positioning, portfolio, catalog, proposal blocks). The material already exists in `fiverr/300_noman-quickbooks`.
  - **Real work from the agency account**: each student does supervised tasks on live client files (categorisation, reconciliation, cleanup segments) under Noman's review. This is the thing no competitor can offer without an agency.
  - Daily job triage drill using the scorecard in `07_client-acquisition.md`; one proposal a day reviewed by Ariful.
  - LinkedIn + Facebook-group routine from `03_social-media-plan.md`.
  - Direct-client sales: how to quote a cleanup, how to convert to monthly.
- Output: a live profile, 1–3 reviewed real tasks on the portfolio, and a decision: agency staff, freelancer, or job-seeker track.

### Phase 3 — Placement track
Three channels, in order of control:
1. **Own agency seats.** Hire the best as junior bookkeepers on the agency (part-time first, Tk 15–25k/month, moving to Tk 25–40k with client load). Client pays $600–1,000 per seat per month [14, 19].
2. **Partner firms.** Supply pre-screened graduates to 10–20 Dhaka/Sylhet offshore firms (list in research file 10). Employer-paid fee: one month's salary or 15–20% of first-year pay [11]. Start the calls in week 1, not after the batch.
3. **Local SME jobs.** Add a Tally module (Tally has 68 Bdjobs mentions vs QuickBooks 24 [14]) for students who want a local accounts job at Tk 20–30k. This is the fallback that keeps a cohort from being "unemployed".

Promise wording (never "job guarantee"): "We publish every cohort's results. Of the last batch of N, X are earning; here is how."

## 4. Unit economics (estimates, Tk; label them as such when quoting)

### Academy, one Phase 1 batch
| Line | Low | Mid |
|---|---|---|
| Students × fee | 20 × 10,000 = 200,000 | 30 × 11,000 = 330,000 |
| Ads + content for the batch | 40,000 | 60,000 |
| Instructor hours (48 h live + 20 h review at Tk 1,500/h) | 102,000 | 102,000 |
| Tools (Zoom, QBO/Xero trial files, LMS/recording, gateway fees ~2%) | 15,000 | 20,000 |
| **Contribution** | **43,000** | **148,000** |

At 20 students the course roughly breaks even after paying the instructor properly. It only makes money at 30+, or when Phase 2 and the agency margin are counted. This is why the course is the funnel, not the business.

### Agency, steady state at month 9–12 (from research 17, Q13)
| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Clients | 8–10 small firms / SMEs, ~$3,500/month in |
| Staff cost (3 juniors from Phase 3) | ~$1,400/month |
| Founders keep | ~$2,000/month, growing with seats |
| Year 2 target | 10 staff, $12,000 in, $5,000 out |

| Channel | Fee |
|---|---|

## 7. Legal and structural minimums before marketing under one brand
- One registered entity (or a written partnership agreement) that owns the brand, the bank/bKash merchant, the student contracts and the client contracts.
- Written student terms: no job guarantee, refund rule (7 days like FinTech BPO), what "placement support" means.
- Data handling rule for client files used in training (anonymised, client consent in writing). US firms are bound by IRS §7216 consent rules for offshoring [22]; this must be clean.
- Remove pirated-software content from every property tied to the instructors.
