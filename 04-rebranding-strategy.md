# 04 — Rebranding Strategy (from zero)

Goal: one brand that a UK accounting-firm owner, a Dhaka BPO manager and a Mirpur BBA graduate all trust on first sight. Today there are three names (HrTech-TutorialsPoint, FinPro Skill Academy, Hamidur Rahman) and none of them does that.

---

## 1. Brand architecture: one master brand, two faces

```
            MASTER BRAND  (company, domain, LinkedIn page, Clutch, Upwork agency)
                 |
     +-----------+-----------+
     |                       |
  [Brand] Academy        [Brand] (services)
  sells to students       sells to foreign firms and SMEs
  Bangla + English        English only
  Facebook, YouTube       LinkedIn, Upwork, Clutch, website
```

Why one brand and not two: the whole pitch is "we train people **and** we do the work". A student trusts an academy that has real clients. A client trusts an agency that has a bench. Splitting the names throws away the only story we have.

Why not keep "FinPro Skill": 326 followers is nothing to protect, the name is generic (hundreds of "FinPro" entities exist worldwide, including US financial firms), and it says "skill" not "work". "HrTech-TutorialsPoint" is a 2018 tutorial blog name and must retire.

Hamidur Rahman stays as a **personal instructor brand** (YouTube face, LinkedIn), the way FinTech BPO and Abacus use their founders. Personal brands pull students; the company brand holds the money and the clients.

## 2. Name: shortlist and how to pick

Criteria: English, 2–3 syllables, says books/ledger, works as a .com and as an Upwork agency name, no existing accounting company with the same name in US/UK/BD, pronounceable in Bangla.

| Candidate | Why | Risk to check |
|---|---|---|
| **LedgerBridge** | "Ledger" = accounting; "Bridge" = Bangladesh to UK/US clients, students to jobs. Academy = LedgerBridge Academy. | Check .com, UK Companies House, USPTO, Facebook handle |
| **ClearLedger** | Cleanup positioning | "ClearBooks" is a UK software; too close |
| **Khata** (খাতা = ledger) + "Khata Partners / Khata Academy" | Bangla identity, memorable abroad | Foreign buyers cannot spell it from hearing; several Indian fintechs use "Khata" |
| **Meridian Books** | Time-zone play (we work while you sleep) | Generic; "Meridian" used widely |
| **NorthBook** | Short, neutral | Weak meaning |

**Working recommendation: LedgerBridge**, subject to the checks below. If it fails, run the same checks on ClearLedger and NorthBook. Do not spend more than one week on the name.

Checks (owner: Ariful, 1 day):
1. Domain: ledgerbridge.com / .co / .io / .com.bd. Buy the .com if available; otherwise pick another name. Do not launch on a .xyz.
2. Trademark: USPTO TESS, UK IPO, DPDT Bangladesh search. We are not filing yet; we are avoiding a conflict.
3. Handles: facebook.com/, youtube.com/@, linkedin.com/company/, instagram, Upwork agency name, Fiverr.
4. Google the name + "accounting" and + "bookkeeping". If page 1 has an active firm, drop it.

## 3. Positioning (write this once, use it everywhere)

**For students (Bangla-first):**
"আমরা কোর্স বেচি না, কাজ শেখাই। আমাদের নিজের ক্লায়েন্টের ফাইলে প্র্যাকটিস, সিনিয়র রিভিউ, আর প্রতিটা ব্যাচের রেজাল্ট প্রকাশ্যে।" (We do not sell a course, we teach the work. Practice on our own client files, senior review, and every batch's results published.)

**For firms (English):**
"UK- and US-trained bookkeepers from Dhaka, reviewed by a qualified accountant before anything reaches you. QuickBooks Online, Xero, cleanup and month-end, from $600 a seat. Two-week free trial."

**One-line brand promise:** *Trained on real books. Reviewed by real accountants. Results published.*

**What we are not:** not a "freelancing" academy, not "earn dollars from home", not a job guarantee, not the cheapest.

## 4. Verbal identity
| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tone | Calm, specific, numbers over adjectives. No "🚀", no "transform your career", no "demanding skill of 2026". |
| Language | Student content: Bangla with English technical terms (the way Hamidur already speaks). Client content: plain English, UK/US spelling matched to the audience. |
| Proof language | Always a number with a source: "Batch 3: 28 enrolled, 24 finished, 11 earning within 90 days." |
| Banned words | guarantee, 100%, unlimited income, secret, hack (except as a video title about an actual software trick), "sir" |
| Instructor naming | "Hamidur Rahman, CMA, QuickBooks & Xero certified" on every slide and thumbnail. Credentials are the differentiator; use them. |

## 5. Visual identity (brief for a designer, or build in Canva in a day)
- **Logo**: wordmark + a simple ledger/bridge glyph. Must survive at 32 px (favicon, YouTube avatar).
- **Colours**: one deep navy (trust, finance), one bright accent (teal or amber) for calls to action, warm off-white background. Avoid the Intuit green and the Xero blue so we do not look like a reseller.
- **Type**: one sans for everything (Inter or IBM Plex Sans), one Bangla face that pairs (Noto Sans Bengali or Hind Siliguri).
- **Photography**: real people at real desks, screen recordings with the brand frame. No stock handshake photos, no AI people.
- **Thumbnail system (YouTube/Reels)**: brand colour block left, 3–5 word Bangla hook, instructor face right, a QBO/Xero screenshot crop. Same template every time so the channel is recognisable in a feed.
- **Templates to make once**: FB post (1:1), Reel/Short (9:16), YouTube thumb (16:9), LinkedIn carousel, certificate, proposal PDF, invoice, email signature, Zoom background.

## 6. Website (replace Blogspot; this is the core asset)
Stack: a simple static site or WordPress on the .com, fast, mobile-first, HTTPS. Ariful can build it in a week. Pages:

| Page | Purpose | Must have |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Route the two audiences | Two doors: "I want to learn" / "I need a bookkeeper". Proof strip: cohort numbers, client count, certifications |
| Academy › Fundamentals course | Convert | **Price on the page**, dates, schedule, syllabus, instructor, selection test link, FAQ, refund terms, "Enrol" button to a gateway (SSLCommerz/bKash merchant/aamarPay), instalment option |
| Academy › Marketplace Hunting | Aspire | Invite-only; how selection works; what students did on real files |
| Academy › Results | Trust | A table per batch: enrolled, finished, earning at 90/180 days, placed. Updated quarterly. Even bad numbers, with a note on what changed. |
| Academy › Free orientation | Capture | Next live date, WhatsApp opt-in, recorded version |
| Services › For accounting firms | Sell seats | Offer, process, security, a 2-week trial, Calendly/booking |
| Services › For small businesses | Sell cleanups | QBO cleanup, monthly bookkeeping, e-commerce integrations (WooCommerce/Shopify × QBO, Ariful's edge) |
| About | Credibility | The three founders, credentials with badge links (Credly, Xero, NSDA), photos |
| Blog / Guides | SEO | Migrate only the 15–20 best accounting posts from hrtecb.com (QBO certification guide, US payroll tax, Xero how-to). Drop everything else. |
| Contact | Convert | WhatsApp, email, address |

Not on the site: Windows tricks, software downloads, graphic design, Amazon affiliate, "Active Windows & Office".

## 8. Launch sequence (so the rebrand itself becomes content)
1. **Soft**: new site live, FB renamed, YouTube re-organised, no announcement. One week to fix bugs.
2. **Founders' story**: three LinkedIn posts and one 6-minute YouTube video: why we are doing this, what we found in the research (use the numbers: 287 applicants per job, 49 entry-level jobs out of 1,169), what we will do differently (published results, real files).
3. **Free orientation class** under the new brand (live, Bangla, 60 min, Hamidur + Ariful), promoted with Tk 10–15k of ads to a WhatsApp opt-in, not to a form.
4. **Batch 1 enrolment** opens the day after the orientation, with the selection test. Early-bird closes in 7 days.
5. **Results promise** posted publicly: "We will publish Batch 1's numbers on [date]." Then do it.
