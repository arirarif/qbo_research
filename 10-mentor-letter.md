# 10 — Mentor's Letter (read when you are tempted to skip steps)

Ariful,

You asked me to be the expert and the mentor, so here is the part that does not fit in a table.

## 1. You already know the answer; the research said it three weeks ago
"Teach QuickBooks and place 200 people" is not a business. "Win clients, train people on those clients' work, hire the best, supply the rest to firms that trust you, and publish what happened" is a business. Hamidur's course and channel do not change that verdict. They make Phase 1 cheaper to start. That is all, and that is useful.

## 2. The order of operations is the whole strategy
1. Agency account and the first two paying clients.
2. Clean the instructor's public footprint.
3. Brand, site, price, gateway, terms.
4. Orientation, selection, Batch 1.
5. Partner-firm agreements, in parallel with 4.
6. Phase 2 only when the agency has work to give students.
7. Publish results. Then repeat.

If you swap 1 and 4 because the course is easier to sell than a client is to win, you become the thirteenth QuickBooks course in Dhaka with a nicer logo.

## 3. Five things to say no to in the first six months
- A third niche. (One for seats, one for cleanups. That is already two.)
- Any ad before the landing page exists.
- Any student who fails the basics test, no matter who refers them.
- Any client job the reviewer has not seen before it ships.
- Any public number you cannot show a source for.

## 4. What each founder must accept
- **Hamidur** gives up the "everything tutorial" identity and the free-download traffic. In return he becomes the named, credentialed face of the one honest accounting academy in the market. His CMA, QBO, Xero and NSDA certificates are worth more than 136K views of a LinkedIn trick.
- **Noman** accepts that his first year is quality and mentoring, not fame. The reviewer role is the thing that makes the agency safe to buy from.
- **You** accept that outbound sales is a scheduled, counted job, 6–9 months before it pays, and that you are the one doing it. Nobody else on the team can.

## 5. How you will know it is working (or not)
- By day 90: paying clients exist, Batch 1 has scores, one firm has signed an intake agreement.
- By month 6: at least 10 of the first 30 students are earning. If fewer, stop marketing Batch 3 and fix the model. Do not "push harder".
- By month 12: 30–60 people working through you, 8–10 clients, four published cohort reports. That is a real company. 200 a year is a year-3 question.

## 6. The one sentence to keep
Trained on real books, reviewed by real accountants, results published. Every decision that does not serve that sentence is a distraction.

## 7. What I need from you to keep going
Answers to file 08. The three that unblock the most: Q-H1 (students so far), Q-H4 (ad spend and registrations), Q-N1/N2 (Noman's profiles and real-file experience). With those, I can turn files 03–07 into the actual selection test, the curriculum v2 outline, the website copy, the first 12 video scripts, the outbound list and sequence, and the partner-firm call script.
