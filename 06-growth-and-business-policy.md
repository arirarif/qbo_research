# 06 — Growth Strategy and Business Policies

Policies are the rules we agree before money or students arrive, so that nobody has to argue about them at 11 pm. Growth is staged with gates: we do not move to the next stage until the gate is met.

---

## Part A. Growth stages and gates

| Stage | Months | What we build | Gate to pass before the next stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 Foundation** | 0–3 | Entity + agreement, brand, website, agency account, piracy clean-up, partner-firm calls, Batch 1 (20–30), first 2–3 clients | Batch 1 finished with results recorded; ≥2 paying clients; 10 partner firms spoken to, ≥3 willing to take candidates |
| **1 Proof** | 3–9 | Batches 2–3, Phase 2 cohort 1, first students on client files, first placements, case studies, Clutch/Upwork reviews, outbound to one niche | ≥10 of the first 30 students earning within 6 months; ≥5 clients; ≥1 seat sold to a firm; CAC under Tk 2,500 |
| **2 Scale the loop** | 9–18 | 4 batches/year, 2 Phase 2 cohorts, own agency desk of 5–10 juniors, 8–10 clients, quarterly results reports, corporate training for firms | $10k+/month agency revenue; 30–60 people working; a senior reviewer besides the founders |
| **3 Institutional** | 18–36 | NSDA/BTEB registered training organisation; apply to the SEIP successor / BACCO FAO channel; university partnerships; Xero partner tier; UK/Sylhet channel; Clutch top-3 in Dhaka; offshore desk 20–40 seats | Government or donor-funded batch won; 2 UK practices on multi-seat contracts |
| **4 Product** | 36+ | Package what we built: the LMS with practice files, the integration tooling (WooCommerce/Shopify × QBO), possibly the real-estate accounting ERP as a product | Recurring software revenue covers one founder's salary |

Rule: if a gate is missed by more than one quarter, we hold a model review (Part C, 3) rather than pushing marketing harder.

## Part B. Growth levers, ranked by evidence
1. **Agency reviews and case studies** (borrowed trust; the only thing that converts both students and clients).
2. **Published cohort results** (no competitor does it; compounding trust).
3. **Partner-firm intake agreements** (placement we control; employer-paid).
4. **Short-form teaching video in Bangla** (cheap reach; the niche's top video is 200K views, so the ceiling is modest but reachable).
5. **One-niche outbound** (slow, 6–9 months, but it is where seat revenue comes from).
6. **Government/NSDA channel** (big batches, paid by someone else; needs the track record first).
7. **Corporate training for Dhaka firms** (revenue plus relationships; uses the credentials).
8. Everything else (conferences, directories, X/Instagram) is year 2+.

## Part C. Governance between the three founders

### 1. Ownership and money (decide in writing; a proposal to react to)
- One entity (proprietorship-to-partnership or a private limited company when revenue justifies it). Trade licence, TIN, bank account, bKash/SSLCommerz merchant in the entity's name.
- Equity: **equal thirds**, vesting over 3 years with a 6-month cliff (if someone leaves in month 4, the others are not stuck). Founder salaries only when monthly contribution allows; until then a role-based draw from profit.
- Profit split by **engine**: Academy profit 50% to the entity reserve, 50% split by teaching/review hours actually delivered. Agency profit 50% reserve, 50% split by sales and delivery hours. This rewards the person doing the work in each engine without renegotiating every month.
- Reserve rule: keep **3 months of fixed costs** in the entity account before any profit distribution.
- The instructor's personal YouTube stays personal property; the brand gets a non-exclusive licence to use the content while he is a founder.

### 2. Decision rights
| Decision | Who decides | Who must be consulted |
|---|---|---|
| Curriculum, assessment, certificates | Hamidur | Noman |
| What goes to a client (quality sign-off) | Noman | — |
| Pricing, discounts, refunds | Ariful | all (policy change needs 2 of 3) |
| Marketing spend up to Tk 50k/month | Ariful | — |
| Spend above Tk 50k, hiring, new partnerships, new products | 2 of 3 | all |
| Brand, website, public claims | Ariful | all |

### 3. Operating rhythm
- **Monday 30 min**: numbers from the dashboard (05, section 4), blockers, three priorities each.
- **Friday 15 min**: what shipped, what slipped.
- **Monthly 2 h**: cohort review, client review, cash, next month's content and ads.
- **Quarterly**: results report published; stage gate check; model review if a gate was missed.

## Part D. Policies

### D1. Admissions
- Selection test required for Phase 1. Minimum: passes the basics section; English paragraph readable. No test, no seat, whatever the fee.
- Max 35 per live batch so review stays real.
- Phase 2 by score + interview only. We say no to people who paid for Phase 1 if they are not ready, and we tell them why and what to do.

### D2. Pricing and discounts
- Prices are public, on the website, in Taka, VAT handled correctly.
- Early-bird (fixed, dated) and instalments are the only discounts. No inbox deals, no "last 3 seats" unless it is true.
- Scholarship: 2 seats per batch for women or candidates from outside Dhaka, by application. Published.
- Corporate training priced per batch, not per head.

### D3. Refunds and promises
- Full refund within 7 days of the first class, no questions.
- No job guarantee, ever, in any channel. "Placement support" means: the Phase 2 track, introductions to partner firms, and agency hiring from the top performers. Written in the student terms.
- Every batch's outcomes published at 90 and 180 days, with the method (how "earning" is defined: at least Tk 10,000 or $100 of paid accounting work in the window).

### D4. Quality (agency)
- Every deliverable is reviewed by Noman or Hamidur before it reaches a client until the staff member has 3 months without a client-raised error.
- Reconciliation reports attached to every month-end. Written summary in plain English with each delivery.
- One named account owner per client; response within one business hour in the client's working day.
- Errors are logged, not hidden; the log is reviewed monthly and feeds the curriculum.

### D5. Data security and confidentiality
- Client files only in the client's own QBO/Xero with role-based user access; no spreadsheets of client data on personal laptops; 2FA on every account; a password manager; no WhatsApp sharing of client documents.
- Training uses anonymised or synthetic files. A client's file is used for training only with written consent (US clients: IRS §7216 consent language).
- NDA signed by every student in Phase 2 and every staff member.
- Offboarding checklist: access removed the same day.

### D6. Client policy
- Contracts for every client (Upwork contract or our own services agreement). Scope, monthly deliverables, deadline (financials by the 10th unless agreed), rate, termination with 30 days' notice.
- Payment: Upwork for Upwork-originated clients (no off-platform migration; it breaks the rules), Wise/Payoneer/bank for direct clients, monthly in advance for seats.
- Trial: 2 weeks free on one bounded task for firm clients; a paid sample batch for SMEs.
- We do not sign tax returns we are not licensed to sign; we prepare and the client's CPA/accountant files.

### D7. Partner-firm policy
- Written intake agreement: what we supply (tested graduates with scores and project files), fee, replacement if the hire leaves within 60 days.
- We never place a student we would not hire ourselves.
- We tell students the firm's real pay band before the interview.

### D8. Hiring and pay (agency staff)
- Hire from our own cohorts first. Part-time first (20 h/week) at Tk 12–18k, full-time Tk 20–30k entry, Tk 30–45k at 2 years, team lead Tk 60k+. Benchmarks from research 10.
- Review at 3 months with the error log. Clear path from Phase 2 student to junior to reviewer.

### D9. Content and claims
- Every number in public content has a source we can show. No invented student numbers, no "42,000 placed".
- No pirated software, cracks, or "free download" content on any brand or founder channel.
- Students appear in content only with written permission; earnings screenshots only with permission and context.

### D10. Finance
- Separate accounts for the entity; no founder personal accounts for student fees.
- Monthly P&L by engine (academy, agency, placement). Yes, in QuickBooks or Xero, so the books themselves are a demo.
- Six months of personal runway for each founder before quitting any other income (research 17: the first 3 months are near zero).

## Part E. Risk register
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Course sells, agency has no work for graduates | High | Fatal to the promise | Agency clients before Phase 2 starts; cap Phase 2 to the seats we can feed |
| First client job done badly (D1 gap) | Medium | Kills the Upwork profile | Senior review on everything; start with small bounded jobs |
| Founder disagreement on money | Medium | Breaks the team | Part C signed before Batch 1 marketing |
| Ad spend with no landing page | High now | Wastes cash | Website and gateway before any ads |
| Piracy history surfaces with a UK firm or Intuit | Medium | Lost deal, partner ban | Clean-up in week 1, unlist videos, remove pages |
| Government free training drains price-sensitive students | High | Lower batch fill | Position above it: selection, real files, results; pursue the funded channel ourselves in stage 3 |
| AI compresses junior bookkeeping rates | High | Margin | Teach review, cleanup, VAT, integrations; sell reviewed output, not hours |
| One founder stops selling | Medium | Revenue stalls | Outbound is a scheduled activity with weekly numbers, not a mood |
