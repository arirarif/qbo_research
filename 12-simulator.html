How the model works (read once)

  • Academy. Each batch: ad spend ÷ cost per registration = registrations, plus organic registrations that grow with every batch. Registrations → test takers → passers → paid (capped by batch size). Fees collected 60% at enrolment, 40% a month later. Costs: ads the month before, instructor hours and tools during the 2-month batch. Finishers = paid × finish rate. Phase 2 takes a share of finishers (capped), charges its fee to the paying half.
  • Agency. SME clients arrive from Upwork/Fiverr at a monthly rate after a ramp; each pays a monthly amount in USD, minus platform fee; churn monthly. Seat clients come from outbound: contacts per month ÷ contacts-per-client, with a lag; each buys N seats at the seat price. Founders deliver the first few clients themselves; beyond that, juniors are needed (SME clients per junior, 1 junior per seat) and a reviewer per 6 juniors.
  • Placement. Juniors are hired first from the Phase 2 pool. Partner firms sign at a quarterly rate (capped) and each hires a yearly number from the pool at a placement fee. Remaining Phase 2 completers freelance with a success rate after a lag; Phase 1 finishers not in Phase 2 freelance or get local jobs at lower rates.
  • Cash. Starting cash + academy + agency + placement − ads − instructor − tools − junior pay − reviewer − founder draw − fixed − one-off launch cost.
