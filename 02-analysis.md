Facts that follow:
1. **Audience.** The leader has roughly 100x our Facebook audience. The YouTube channel is the only asset in the same order of magnitude as the mid-tier (BONGO IT 4.16K, Nahid 5.66K), but its subscribers came from LinkedIn-verification and free-download videos, not from accounting content.
2. **Price.** Tk 8,000 sits in the low-middle band (workshops 2–8k, full courses 10–20k, premium BPO 25–33k [11]). It is priced like a workshop while delivering a 40-hour course. That is a margin problem, not a demand advantage: at 20 students a batch it is Tk 160,000 gross for 40 live hours plus support, and after a discount "announced in inbox" it is less.
3. **Credentials.** Hamidur's stack (CMA, QBO certified, Xero Advisor, Gusto, NSDA L4) plus Noman's (MBA Accounting, auditor, QB L1/L2) is stronger than most of the long tail and equal to the leaders. This is real and under-used.
4. **Proof.** Nobody in the market publishes verified outcomes [11, section 7]. Neither do we. The first team that publishes honest cohort numbers owns the trust position.

## 2. What works in Hamidur's current setup
- The **syllabus is better than most**: both QBO (US) and Xero (UK VAT, UK payroll), bank reconciliation, reports, plus marketplace setup and a practical assessment. The two-country design matches the research (UK Xero work is the least crowded [23]).
- **No job promises.** Honest, and it is the right legal position in a market with scam history (UpSpot arrests [11]).
- **Recordings, templates, a support class, a demo-business exercise.** These are real delivery features.
- **NSDA Level 4 certificate** on the instructor. Nobody else in our team has a government competency credential. It is the route to becoming an NSDA/SEIP-type registered provider later.
- **He has taught at LCBS**, so he already has a third-party stage credit.
- The reel got 42.8K views. Whatever the spend, the creative and the hook ("Freelancing in Accounting") pull attention.

## 5. The research verdict still holds, and the new assets do not change it
- A student-paid course cannot fill 100+ seats a batch in year 1; plan **20–40** [21].
- Entry-level accounting work is shrinking; the work that exists is cleanup, month-end, reconciliation, UK VAT, niche verticals [14, 15].
- Placement only works when the trainer **controls the hiring channel**: own agency seats, partner firms, government-funded batches [11, 14].
- Cold outreach to foreign firms is a 6–9 month grind at thousands of contacts per client [19].

What the new assets **do** add:
- A running course and an instructor, so Phase 1 does not have to be built from zero.
- A credential (NSDA L4) that opens the government channel.
- A YouTube channel with 3.8K subscribers that can be re-pointed.
- A second senior accountant for review and delivery.

## 6. The three real risks of this partnership
1. **Three people, no shared company.** Who owns the brand, the client contracts, the student money? Decide before the first batch is marketed under one name (06, Q-T1).
2. **Selling the course before the agency exists.** If students finish in January 2027 and there is no agency account taking work, we have the same failure as everyone else. The agency and the first 3–5 clients must be running **before** the "Marketplace Hunting" cohort starts.
3. **Reputation contamination.** Pirated-software pages and "free download" videos attached to the instructor's name will surface the first time a UK firm or Intuit checks us. Clean-up is a precondition, not a nice-to-have.

## 7. The edge we actually have (use it in every pitch)

- **UK Xero + US QBO in one syllabus**, already written.
